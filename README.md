## Selected projects

**[forex-medium-frequency-trading](https://github.com/OptiFabian/forex-medium-frequency-trading)**
An out-of-sample comparison on six major currency pairs and 15-minute bars, under realistic bid/ask costs, in which a filtered Bollinger-band mean-reversion strategy beat an XGBoost model trained on triple-barrier labels. The strategy's own edge is modest: positive on the held-out quarters but concentrated in one of them. Python.

**[BPC-Stochastic-Crowd-VRP](https://github.com/OptiFabian/BPC-Stochastic-Crowd-VRP)**
A Branch-Price-and-Cut algorithm for vehicle routing with a mixed fleet of professional and crowd-sourced vehicles, based on a set-covering master problem. Routes are generated by an ng-route labeling algorithm, strengthened with rounded capacity cuts, and branched on vehicle counts and arcs. Java with CPLEX.

**[LNS-Crowdshipping-VRPTW](https://github.com/OptiFabian/LNS-Crowdshipping-VRPTW)**
A Large Neighborhood Search with simulated annealing for the vehicle routing problem with time windows, where some routes go to crowd drivers whose availability is uncertain and a professional vehicle serves the route at a higher price if the driver does not show up. It minimizes the expected total cost and is written in plain Java, tested on Solomon instances.

**[Discretization-for-VRPTW](https://github.com/OptiFabian/Discretization-for-VRPTW)**
My Julia/JuMP implementation of the LA-Discretization method of Mandal, Regan, Rousseau and Yarkony (2024), an exact compact formulation for the vehicle routing problem with time windows. It runs on the free HiGHS solver and reaches the proven optima on the tested Solomon instances.
